# A model alone on the holdout tracebacks: results of 2026-11-15, with cheaper models (DT-QA-19)

`results-2026-11-14.md` has the three Claude models (DT-QA-16). This adds two cheaper models from other vendors, asked
through OpenRouter under `cheaper-models.md` (protocol `d3d002e`; the trial's changes `cb9a0c1` and `9f3f31f`, both
committed before any run): DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (`deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash`, released 2026-09-10) and Qwen3.5 9B
(`qwen/qwen3.5-9b`, released 2026-03-10), one run each, 191 calls each, none failed, every reply finished. They cost
$0.91 and $0.03. The Claude replies are the same files as on 2026-11-14, scored again unchanged. Everything below
"Every table" is `grade.py`'s output.

## Reading it

- **DepTime's lead grows as the model gets smaller or cheaper:** 3 to 5 points over Opus and Sonnet, about 20 over
  Haiku, 28 over DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and 68 over Qwen3.5 9B.
- **DeepSeek V4.1 Flash names the change about as often as Haiku** (92.7% of root causes) **and the replacement
  nearly as often** (84.7%), but gets the release right far less often: 35.6% where the question doesn't give it away.
  It reasons at length (a mean of 7,288 thinking tokens a call) and answers in 32 seconds on average.
- **Qwen3.5 9B is the first model DepTime beats on every measure,** replacements included (22.4% against 48.2%). It
  answers at once, without reasoning (41 thinking tokens on average), and gets even the package wrong in 12 cases and
  the name in 80. Reading `Class(param)` as `Class.__init__(param)`, which the prompt asks for, would rescue one case.
- **The "break released after" column** dates breaks against Claude's June 2026 cutoff; Qwen3.5 9B was released in
  March 2026, so it can't know breaks from April on either.
