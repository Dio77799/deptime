## Reading it

- **Sonnet alone comes close to DepTime here, as on the tracebacks.** It finds 9 in 10 breaks and almost never
  flags working code. Its intervals overlap DepTime's (85.1–94.4% against 92.5–98.6% for breaks found), and the
  breaks sit in 27 upgrades, which makes the real uncertainty wider.
- **Haiku falls well behind,** as it did on the tracebacks: 23 points fewer breaks found than DepTime, a third of the
  broken upgrades with none found, and false alarms in 3 clean upgrades (weaviate-client 3 → 4's `Client` and
  `AuthApiKey`, which 4.x keeps, and urllib3's `_set_socket_options` going down). When it gives a verdict it is
  mostly right; much of the gap is `unknown`.
- **Neither leans on the version numbers.** Every rule that knows nothing and finds a break flags a fifth or more
  of the working uses; the models flag 5 and 7 of 2,113.
- **Going down was Sonnet's strongest direction** (95.2% of 63 breaks): names not yet added need only the older
  release's API, which predates its training cutoff. Haiku: 77.8% down, 70.8% up.
- **The training cutoff can't be tested well on this set.** 97 breaks sit in upgrades whose newer release is from
  after June 2026, but most of those breaks were made by older releases. Dated by the release that made them (after
  the results, last section), 21 breaks come from after the cutoff, in three upgrades: `mcp` 2.0.0 (2026-07-28) up and
  down, and `anthropic` 1.0.0 (2026-08-20). Sonnet found 19 of the 21 (all but `anthropic`'s `AI_PROMPT` and
  `HUMAN_PROMPT`), Haiku 9.
- **Where Sonnet misses:** `langchain-community`'s removed `vectorstores.Qdrant` (4 uses), `langgraph` 0.2.39's
  missing `errors.GraphBubbleUp` and `get_checkpoint_metadata`, scikit-learn's compiled `tree._tree.DTYPE`, the two
  `anthropic` constants and `crewai`'s `Crew.kickoff(inputs=)` before it existed, and it answers `unknown` on fastmcp
  4's `prompts.prompt` (4 uses). DepTime's five misses are those fastmcp uses and one `langgraph` one.
